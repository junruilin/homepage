I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at The University of Texas at Austin. My research is in industrial organization and health economics, with a focus on selection markets and mechanism design. I study how insurance contracts trade off reclassification risk against moral hazard, and how market structure shapes pricing and consumer choice.
