I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at The University of Texas at Austin. My research is in industrial organization and health economics. I study how public and private financing shape innovation in health care, and how insurance markets work, from adverse selection within households to the consequences of the 2026 expiration of insurance subsidies.
